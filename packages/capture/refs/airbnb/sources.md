# Airbnb: sources

Airbnb publishes no design tokens as code, but every page ships its DLS palette as CSS variables (`--palette-*`), so colors come from those and sizes from computed styles.

## 2026-10-21

All public, signed out, headless Chromium at 1440×900, DPR 2, light only (`prefers-color-scheme` doesn't switch the guest site). Raw measures and screenshots are in the project files under `presets/airbnb/2026-10-21/`. Probe 8eb9b12.

- **Pages.** `www.airbnb.com/` (tabs, the header's quiet and icon buttons, the main menu, the login dialog's field, the search panel's Dates | Flexible switch), `/s/Paris/homes` (the page numbers; with Filters open, the ink confirm, the dialog and the amenity chips) and a public listing, `/rooms/47803806` (soft secondary button, focus ring, the booking card, calendar days, headings, body and muted text), plus the search cards' tags (`badge`) and the host landing page's links (`/host/homes`). Nothing in the pack reads listing or host content beyond the element's style.
- **Breadth.** The experiences search, the help center and the host landing page were scanned too; they add no part the pack lacks beyond the tags and links (the help center's "Guest" dropdown is a 34px filter pill, not a form select; the login dialog no longer has a country select). Viewport screenshots of all six public pages are in the project files under `presets/airbnb/2026-10-21/pages/`. Signed-in pages are in 2026-10-22.
- **Hydration.** Airbnb renders before React attaches, so clicks do nothing for 3–4s after `load`. Every page settles 4000ms; Filters, the profile menu and the login dialog open with `focus` + `press: Enter`, and the When field and the Wifi chip take a `click` after the settle. The public pages run with `--pack`; the signed-in ones are merged. `input.focus` focuses the field's input in a `before` step: `focus: true` on the part did not trigger Airbnb's focus style. `toggle.on` clicks the chip instead of pressing Enter, which would leave a focus ring on it.
- **Font stand-in.** `tokens.json` maps Cereal (`airbnb cereal vf`) to Plus Jakarta Sans, so `fontFamily` rows read as stand-in.
- **Palette.** `:root` of the HTML holds the light palette, `client.css` the `-dark` set the guest site never applies. Light: page `#ffffff`, ink `#222222`, muted `#6c6c6c`, hairline `#dddddd` (`border-secondary`), field edge `#8c8c8c`, soft fill `#f2f2f2` / `#f7f7f7`, track `#ebebeb`, error `#c13515` on `#fff5f3`, success `#038026`, warning `#eb6100`, scrim black 30%.
- **Dark is token-derived.** The guest site ships no dark mode, so `dark` in `tokens.json` is written by hand from the `-dark` palette, on the light entries' props, with clear fills composited over the page and no shadows (the `-dark` set has none): page and overlays `#111111`, ink `#f7f7f7`, values `#efefef`, muted `#a6a6a6`, hairline `#2c2c2c`, field edge `#6a6a6a`, soft fill `#222222`, inverse `#f7f7f7`, scrim black 60%.
- **Brand.** Rausch `#ff385c` is the logo and `bg-primary-core`. The CTAs (Reserve, login Continue, Search) are a gradient: `#e61e4d → #e31c5f → #d70466` in light, `#ff385c → #ff497d` in dark; the price bars and the gradient's midpoint are `#e31c5f`, the search icon `#da1249`. The probe reads `background-color` only, so the gradient is recorded here, not in `tokens.json`.
- **Two primaries.** Dialog confirms ("Show 1,000+ places") are ink `#222222` / `#f7f7f7` at 40px, r12, 14/500, 20px padding; conversion CTAs are the Rausch gradient at 48px, 16/500. `button.primary` measures the ink confirm.
- **Weights against the stand-in font.** Cereal is proprietary. Rendered side by side in a canvas, Cereal 500 carries 3.50 px of ink per px of width at 14px; Plus Jakarta Sans 500 carries 3.11, 600 carries 3.58 (5.53 vs 5.64 at 22px). So the preset's 600 labels and headings match Cereal's 500, and the `fontWeight` rows stay off by 100 on purpose.
- **Skips.** `height` and `fontSize` where the part's size is set by its page (headings, the secondary button's long label), the tabs' box (the label span is measured), the quiet button's radius (clear at rest, a pill only on hover), padding on the field (the label and input pad different boxes), `boxShadow` on fields (the edge is an inset shadow; `edge` compares it), the dialog's `edge` (a 2% black ring, `#fafafa` on white) and the card's fill (the booking card sits on the page).
- **Segmented, two styles.** `segmented` measures the search panel's Dates | Flexible switch: a 44px `#ebebeb` track at 100px corners, 36px chips in ink 14/500, the selected one white inside a 1px `#dddddd` edge, no shadow. The Filters dialog's Type of place is the other style: a 1px `#dddddd` r16 track, 54px, whose selected cell is `#f7f7f7` with a 2px `#222222` edge at r12, drawn by a sibling element, so it is noted here and not in the pack.
- **Chips.** `toggle` is the Filters dialog's Wifi chip: 48px, r28, 16px padding, 14/400, white in a 1px `#dddddd` edge. On, the fill stays white and the edge turns ink, drawn as a 1px border plus a 1px inset ring; `edge` reads the border's 1px, `boxShadow` the ring.
- **Measured, not in the pack** (no specimen part): the number stepper's 32px `#f2f2f2` circles; calendar days at 48px in the search panel (the pack reads the listing's 42px), past days grayed, single-letter weekdays; tags 20px, r6, 11/500, `#6c6c6c` on `#f2f2f2`; tabs 14/500 with a 3px `#222222` bar under the icon and label and no rule under the list; menu rows 36px at 14/400 with 24px inline padding and separators inset 24px; dialogs 568px wide with a centered title.
- **Tags.** `badge` is a search card's "Free cancellation" tag: 20px, 6px corners, 4px padding, 11/500, `#6c6c6c` on `#f2f2f2` at 75% (read as `#f5f5f5` over white); dark is derived from the `-dark` palette. Experiences show the success variant, `#038026` on `#e6f6e9` at 75%. The accent badge is signed in (see 2026-10-22).
- **Not measured.** `field.label`: the login field's label floats inside the field at 16/400 `#6c6c6c`, not above it. No public radio or progress bar.
- **No public checkbox or switch.** The AI search toggle is a custom glass switch, not the DLS one; the checkbox, radio and switch come from the signed-in pages below.

## 2026-10-22, signed in

Mehdi's own account, read in a local Claude Code session on his Mac (Chrome over CDP, emulated 1440×900 at DPR 2), light only: the web app has no appearance setting, so nothing was switched and nothing needed restoring. Computed styles of UI chrome only; no names, listings, messages or prices were read or kept. Raw bundles, notes and 17 cropped references are in the project files under `presets/airbnb/2026-10-22/`. Probe 8eb9b12; each page was saved and `--merge`d. `dark` for these parts is derived by hand like the rest: page `#111111`, ink `#f7f7f7`, soft `#222222`, field edge `#6a6a6a`, the accent badge on `bg-brand-secondary` `#311b2d` under `icon-brand` `#ff385c`.

- **Pages.** `/account-settings` (opens Personal information: the page title, the side nav, its pink "New" badge), `/account-settings/privacy-and-sharing` (`Escape` closes an announcement shown on every load; section titles, the on switch, the profile-visibility dialog's radios), `/account-settings/notifications` (the Edit dialog's off switch, the marketing checkbox). Surveyed with nothing new for the pack: preferences, language and currency, wishlists, trips, the host dashboard, listings, insights.
- **Headings.** The type sits on the title's child span (`h1 > span`, 26/600, -0.52px) and on Privacy's section `h3` (22/600, -0.44px), both -0.02em; `fontSize` is skipped like the public headings. These are Cereal 600, a step above the listing titles' 500, so the preset's 600 headings sit between the two. The probe compares `letterSpacing` in px across the specimen's larger sizes, so -0.025em reads -0.4px / -0.3px off where it is -0.005em.
- **Side nav.** `nav.item` is the account-settings side nav: 56px rows, 16px padding, 16/400 in full ink; the current row is a `#f2f2f2` chip at 16px corners, 16/500 (the fill is on the link's parent, with `ink` on the label). The idle rows' radius (clear at rest) and the chip's padding (on the inner link) are skipped. The host dashboard's top nav is tab-like instead: 40px links at 14/500 in `#6c6c6c`, the current one `#222222` over a separate 1.5px ink underline.
- **Accent badge.** `badge.accent` is the side nav's "New": a full pill at 20px, 8px padding, 11/500, `#d80669` (`icon-brand`) on `#fbeff8` (`bg-brand-secondary`), while the neutral tags round at 6px.
- **Selection controls.** Switches paint a 48×32 track (on `#222222`, off `#8c8c8c`) with a 28px white knob. The checkbox is a 22px box at 6px corners under a 1px `#8c8c8c` edge (its input is 1px; the box is the sibling span). Radios are 22px with the same edge; checked, a 2px `#222222` inset ring around a 14px `#222222` dot, on white. The probe reads the checked ring as `edge: none`, so `radio.checked` skips `edge`. No checked checkbox could be read without changing a setting.
- **Measured, not in the pack.** The host calendar marks today with a 32px Rausch circle (`#da1249`, white 18/500); the specimen calendar is a date picker, and Airbnb's guest date picker marks nothing, so the pack keeps the guest picker. The calendar's "Month" dropdown is a 40px `#f2f2f2` pill at 14/500, a toolbar filter like the help center's, not a form select. The listing status chip ("Unlisted") is a white 32px pill at 40px corners, 12/500. Settings tabs (14/500, `#6c6c6c` / `#222222`) repeat the public tabs; the help link repeats the public link (`#222222`, 500, underlined).
- **Host terms dialog.** Every host page opens a "Review our updated terms" dialog that can't be dismissed without accepting or declining, so host crops are dimmed and hover-only parts (tooltips) can't be reached there.
- **Not found.** A checked checkbox, progress bars, alerts and tooltips.
