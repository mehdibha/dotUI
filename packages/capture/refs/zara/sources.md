# Zara: sources

Zara publishes no tokens as code, but every page of www.zara.com ships its design system, ZDS (`zds-` classes), with its semantic palette and type ramp as CSS variables. Colors come from those, sizes from computed styles.

## 2026-10-23

US store in English (`www.zara.com/us/en/`), signed out, light only. zara.com blocks headless browsers, so every page was read in a regular Chrome on Mehdi's Mac through the local capture kit (`www/scripts/local-capture/`), emulated at 1440×900, DPR 2, and each page's readings were `--merge`d. The pages are public; `signedIn` only keeps `--pack` from loading them headless. Raw bundles, notes, 30 viewport screenshots and 53 cropped references are in the project files under `presets/zara/2026-10-23/`. Probe ce94064.

- **Pages.** Home (the region dialog shown on first visit, and the menu drawer), a women's listing (side nav, product cards, the view switch, Filters), a men's listing with the filters drawer open, a product page (Add with keyboard focus, the size selector, headings and copy), the bag with one item, search, the login (on account.zara.com, a second system with `mds-` classes and the same look) and the newsletter page. The help center, stores and gift card pages were surveyed and add nothing the pack lacks. The product page key is a placeholder: products go out of stock, any in-stock product page reads the same.
- **No dark mode.** The web store ignores `prefers-color-scheme` and has no theme switch; its only dark rules target native-app webviews. ZDS still ships a full `.zds-theme--dark` set, so `dark` in `tokens.json` is written from it on the light entries' props: page `#000000`, ink `#ffffff`, mid `#929292` (light `#757575`; the login's `#6b6b6b` label and `#797979` field line map there too), low `#666666` (light `#cccccc`), primary fill white with black text, focus `#0084ff` (light `#0170e9`). No shadows in either set.
- **Palette** (`:root`): page `#ffffff`, ink `#000000`, mid `#757575`, low `#cccccc`, `background-low` `#f7f7f7`, `background-mid` `#949494`; danger `#e90d01`, success `#0a882a`, warning `#b66009`, info and focus `#0170e9`, sales `#23f444`; overlays `rgba(0,0,0,.25)` and `overlay-alt`/`surface` `rgba(255,255,255,.75)`.
- **Font stand-in.** Helvetica Now Text, mostly at 300, with 500 for the current item. The builder loads 400 to 700 only, so the stand-in is picked at 400: rendered in a canvas against Helvetica Now Text 300, Work Sans 400 matches its caps width (23.0 vs 23.2 em per line) and is the lightest of the wide grotesques (ink 0.161 vs 0.131). `tokens.json` maps it, so `fontFamily` rows read as stand-in; `fontWeight` rows stay off by 100 (300 vs 400) on purpose.
- **Type ramp** (`--font-size-*`, all weight 300, tracking 0): title-l 18/28, title-m 15/24, body-l 13/20, body-m 12/18, body-s 10/16, labels 13, 12, 11 and 10 on a 16px line. Buttons, fields and toggles are 13px, copy 12px, product names and section titles 11px, tags 10px. Buttons, nav, tabs, headings, field labels, tags and toggles are set in capitals with `text-transform: uppercase` at normal tracking; copy and menu rows stay as written.
- **Buttons.** `button.primary` is the region dialog's ZDS primary: black, 32px, 12px padding, square, 13/300. The bag's CONTINUE and the login's Continue read the same. `button.secondary` is the newsletter's Subscribe: white inside a 0.5px black line, 32px; the region dialog's second button reads the same. The product page's Add is ZDS's large secondary: 40px, the same 0.5px black line. `button.quiet` is the listing's Filters, bare text; `button.icon` the product card's "+", 32px, no edge.
- **Focus.** Rings show only once the site has seen a real key press (it swaps `theme--mouse-user` for `theme--keyboard-user` on `body`); `button.focus` was read with the class swapped by hand: a 2px `#0170e9` outline 2px off the edge. Fields show focus as a 2px `#0170e9` bottom line.
- **Fields.** `input`, `input.focus` and `field.label` are the login's email field: bottom line only, 0.5px `#797979`, 40px, 13/300; 2px `#0170e9` when focused; the label is 12px `#6b6b6b` above the value. The www field (help search, newsletter) is the same underline at 32px, 0.5px `#757575`, drawn by a separate element the probe can't read.
- **Checkboxes.** The bag's item checkbox is an SVG: a 12px square outline, plus a 4px filled square inside when checked, in black; `edge` is skipped because the box draws no border.
- **Toggles.** `toggle` and `toggle.on` are text options in the filters drawer (sort, size): no box; on is 500 weight with +0.8px tracking.
- **Segmented.** The listing's VIEW 1 / 2 / 3: bare numbers, 24px, no track; the current one reads the same in computed style and differs only in opacity (shown in the crop).
- **Tabs.** WOMAN, MAN, KIDS in the menu drawer are tabs in `"Times New Roman"` at 28px, -1.5px; the current one has a separate bullet dot, no underline or fill.
- **Nav.** The listing's side nav: 20px rows, 13/300 in black, the current row 500, no fill or bar. In the screenshots the whole left column (side nav, Filters, VIEW) shows gray, at #8b8b8b in one shot and #a8a8a8 in another, while the header beside it is black: an entrance fade the capture tab froze part-way. Each element reads `#000` at opacity 1, so the pack keeps black.
- **Overlays.** The region dialog: white, a 1px `#cccccc` line, square, no shadow, on a `rgba(255,255,255,.75)` wash with no blur. The filters, size guide and added-to-bag drawers share the same white wash and have no edge or shadow. The size selector (`menu`) is a 0.5px black frame with 20px rows at 12/300.
- **Tags.** `badge` is the product card's NEW: plain 10/300 text, no box.
- **Measured, not in the pack** (no specimen part): field error text 10/300 `#e90d01` under a 0.5px `#e90d01` line; search combobox 33px, 12/300, no box; separators 0.5px black; filter color swatches 12px squares in a 0.5px `#cccccc` line that turns black when checked; the price range, a 1px black line over `#949494` with native thumbs; the bag quantity stepper (− 1 +), bare glyphs 32px tall; the menu drawer's rows 15/300 at 36px and its group labels 14/300; the help center's list rows 12/400.
- **Not found.** Selects (the store uses drawers and comboboxes), radios, switches, pagination (listings scroll without end), tooltips, progress bars, alerts, cards with a surface, keyboard hints, dates.
