# Pinterest: sources

Pinterest's open-source Gestalt stopped at `gestalt` 177.0.12 (December 2025); pinterest.com now runs a newer, internal "calico" theme. Its full token set ships in the web app's bundle, so colors, radii and shadows come from that, and sizes from computed styles on the live site.

## 2026-10-23

All public, signed out, headless Chromium at 1440×900, DPR 2. Full-page shots of every page below (light and dark), the crops, the compare sheets and the raw token objects are in the project files under `presets/pinterest/2026-10-23/`. Probe ce94064.

- **Pages in the pack.** The Pinterest profile `/pinterest/` (Follow, Log in and share buttons, the board titles, bio and handle, the nav rail, the "More options" tooltip and its flyout), `/ideas/` (the login modal, the page's 36/28 headings, the "Join for free" link), a public pin (`/pin/309692911902891416/`, the header's transparent Log in), `/business/create/` (the email field, its label and focus), a search (`/search/pins/?q=latte nails`, the search guide chips) and `trends.pinterest.com` (the native select, the "New" badge, the trend cards, 20px section titles).
- **Breadth.** Also crawled in both modes and kept as full-page shots: the home page, the today and shopping pages, two ideas categories, a board, `/login/`, `/signup/`, `/password/reset/`, `/business/login/`, business.pinterest.com, a Trends moment and the help center. They add no part the pack lacks: every surface is the same handful of Gestalt pieces (48px r16 buttons, 48px r16 fields with a `#91918c` edge, r16 search chips, r16/r20 overlays).
- **Tokens.** `https://s.pinimg.com/webapp/35330-b4d6642d0e6fa583.mjs` (content-hashed, retrieved 2026-10-23) holds two flat objects of about 2,600 CSS custom properties each: the light and dark calico themes (`base-*`, `sema-*`, `comp-*`). `DesignTokensProvider` writes the light one on `:root`; the dark one is written only in dark mode, which pinterest.com offers signed-in behind an experiment. Values the preset uses: brand `#e60023`; selection, checks, switches, sliders and focus `#435ee5` (dark `#677eee`/`#92a4ff`); warm neutrals at OKLCH h≈107 (`#f6f6f3`, `#e5e5e0`, `#c8c8c1`, `#91918c`, `#62625b`, `#494943`, `#33332e`); ink `#000000` / `#ffffff`; success `#068440`, warning `#db5b06`, error `#dd0e0e`; roundings 4·8·12·16·20·24·32; shadows surface `0 .5px 3px .2`, raised `0 1.5px 6px .2`, floating `0 3px 12px .2`.
- **Dark is rendered with Pinterest's own dark tokens.** Signed-out pages never switch theme, so the dark pass loads each pack page with the bundle's dark object injected on `:root` (plus `body { background-color: var(--sema-color-background-default) }`), then measures and crops it like the light pass. Components that branch on the color scheme in JavaScript rather than in CSS would not switch; none in the pack do. The pass uses a scratch copy of `measure-preset.mts` with a CSS injection; the light pass is plain `--pack`.
- **Selected chip.** Signed out, every guide click opens the login modal, so `toggle.on` renders the product's own selected classes on the first plain chip (`colorBase_11` → `selected_11`, `textColorDefault_11` → `textColorSelected_11`) before measuring: `#33332e` and white in light, `#c8c8c1` and black in dark, 44px inside the chip's 2px margin.
- **Hydration.** The profile ignores clicks for a few seconds after `load`: it settles 4000ms before the More options flyout opens. The ideas page's login modal arrives late and is an A/B test (a "redesign" with one email button, or a "streamlined" email + password form), so the page waits for `[role=dialog][aria-label=modal] > div` and reads only what both share: the panel, the headings and "Join for free".
- **Font stand-in.** `tokens.json` maps Pin Sans to Figtree: rendered side by side at 56/40/32px, Figtree has the same single-story g, straight-tailed y and flat-based 2, and within 2% of its width. Older surfaces (Trends) fall back to the system stack.
- **Two button sizes.** Consumer surfaces use Gestalt's lg: 48px, r16, 14px padding, 16/500. Forms and upsells drop to md: 36px, r12, 10px padding, 14/500; sm is 32px at r8. The pack measures lg, the preset's md, at touch density.
- **Focus.** Buttons draw a 2px white (dark: black) inner border and a 2px `#435ee5` outline at offset 0, which reads as `edge` + `ring +0px`; the preset's 2px ring after a 2px gap draws the same pixels. Fields drop their edge for a 2px `#435ee5` outline, read as `ring +0px`; the preset's 2px border line draws the same.
- **Skips.** `color` on the page, dialog and card (the legacy `#211922` body color; Gestalt text is `#000000`), `paddingInline` on the tooltip and chips (padding sits on a child), `boxShadow` on the menu (the flyout's `0 3px 12px .2` sits on a wrapper without the fill; the dialog measures the same shadow), `height` and `borderRadius` on the Trends select (an older 40px r8 native select; the consumer `SelectList` is 48px r16). `badge.accent` keeps only shape and type: Pinterest has no brand-colored badge, and the measured "New" is the info badge (`#2b48d4` on `#ebeeff`, dark `#92a4ff` on `#17287d`).
- **Menu.** The only public flyout is the signed-out "More options" one: a 220px r16 panel with 8px padding and one Terms of service row, 28px, r8, 14/500. Dropdown rows proper are signed in; the bundle's `comp-dropdown-rounding-*` are 16/12/8, and gestalt 177.0.12's `Dropdown.Item` under the main theme is `TextUI` md (16/500) with 12px × 8px padding and r8, about 40px: the preset's rows match all but the weight.
- **Inferred from tokens** (no public instance): checkbox (unchecked `#91918c` 1px edge, checked `#435ee5`, r8 at md, r4 at sm), radio (filled `#435ee5` with a white dot), switch (off `#91918c`, on `#435ee5`, white 22/20px knob), slider (track `#e5e5e0`, gray `#74746c` range, `#435ee5` knob with a 2px white border), tabs (clear, black underline under the current one, labels black in both states), segmented control (`#e5e5e0` track, `#33332e` selected segment with white text, r16/r12 at lg), toast (`#494943` with white text, r16), progress (`#677eee` on `#f6f6f3`), callouts (tinted fills, no edge, r16), skeleton (`#e5e5e0` with a `#f6f6f3` highlight).
- **Not public.** Pinterest's own dark mode, settings, dropdown menus, tabs and the other selection controls are signed in only.
