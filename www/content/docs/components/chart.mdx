Every chart type — [area](/docs/components/chart-area), [bar](/docs/components/chart-bar), [line](/docs/components/chart-line), [pie](/docs/components/chart-pie), [radar](/docs/components/chart-radar), [radial](/docs/components/chart-radial), and [heatmap](/docs/components/chart-heatmap) — renders through one `chart` core built on [TanStack Charts](https://tanstack.com/charts). The core owns what they have in common, so this page covers it once; each chart's page covers its own options. For ready-made compositions, browse the [chart examples](/docs/charts).

## Installation

Installing any chart brings the core along. Add it on its own only to build a chart type of your own:

```npm
npx shadcn@latest add @dotui/chart
```

## Colors

Series are painted from an eight-slot palette, `--chart-1` to `--chart-8`, in the order the chart lists them — `y` fields, or `seriesOrder` for long data. A ninth series wraps back to the first slot.

The palette is generated by your design system's color engine, for light and dark: tonal shades of your accent by default, or hues spread around it — pick the strategy in the [studio](/studio). Axes, grid, and tooltip read the same semantic tokens as the rest of your components, so a chart never needs colors of its own.

## Size

A chart fills its container's width and tracks resizes. Its height defaults to 16:9 of that width — set `height` in pixels or a different `aspectRatio` to change it, and `className` to size or place the outer box. Below 420px wide, the category axis thins its ticks.

## Frame

Cartesian charts share the same frame props:

- `axes` shows both axes, neither, or just `"x"` or `"y"`. Defaults to `"x"`.
- `grid` toggles the value-axis grid lines. Whether they're solid or dashed is a design-system choice, set in the studio.
- `legend` adds a color legend below the plot. Keep it on whenever a chart has more than one series.
- `formatX` and `formatY` format tick labels and tooltip values.

## Tooltip and focus

Pointer and keyboard share one focus model. `focus` decides what a position resolves to: `"group-x"` (the default) highlights every series at that position, `"nearest"` a single point. `tooltipAnchor` pins the tooltip to the focused point, the pointer, or the center of the focused group.

`onFocusChange` and `onSelect` report the focused and activated point — build a linked detail view or a drill-down on them. `renderTooltipBody` replaces the tooltip's body and receives the default one to wrap or discard.

## Motion

Charts animate between data states with a spring, and play an entrance on first paint. Pass `animate={false}` to turn it off, or a `{ type: "tween" }` or `{ type: "spring" }` transition to tune it. Charts past about 800 points skip animation, and reduced motion is always respected.

Data and mark layers are compared by identity: keep `data`, `marks`, and `marksBefore` out of the render body, or memoize them, so an update animates instead of rebuilding.

## Accessibility

- `ariaLabel` is required — a chart is a figure. Describe what it measures, not its type, and add `ariaDescription` when the takeaway needs a sentence.
- The chart surface is in the tab order. Arrow keys walk the points, `Home` and `End` jump to the ends, `Enter` and Space pin the tooltip and fire `onSelect`, and `Escape` dismisses it.
- The tooltip doubles as a live region, so screen readers announce each focus stop. `tooltip={false}` keeps the stops but silences them — use it only on a decorative chart.
- Color alone never carries meaning: pass `labels` so every series has a name in the legend, the tooltip, and the announcement.
