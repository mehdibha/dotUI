# Supabase: sources

Supabase's `ui` package is open source ([supabase/supabase](https://github.com/supabase/supabase), `packages/ui`), and supabase.com/design-system renders it live. Values come from computed styles on the signed-in dashboard (Studio) and on those pages, cross-checked against the theme files.

## 2026-10-21 and 2026-10-22

`tokens.json` (probe 8eb9b12, 1440×900, light and dark) comes from 12 pages of supabase.com/design-system, measured headless on 2026-10-21 (`presets:measure --pack supabase`, both modes through `prefers-color-scheme`), and 8 signed-in dashboard pages measured in Mehdi's Chrome (see Dashboard). Reference images, specimens and raw measures are in the project files under `presets/supabase/2026-10-21/` and `presets/supabase/2026-10-22/`; the dashboard's full-page shots show account names and stay on Mehdi's Mac.

- **Theme files**: `packages/ui/build/css/themes/light.css` and `dark.css` define a runtime OKLCH theme on hue 157.5: `--surface` 0.995 / 0.19 with an `--elevation-step` of 0.024 / 0.025, `--foreground-lightness` 0.1 / 0.95 with muted and tertiary levels (0.65 / 0.8, 0.5 / 0.65), and `--primary` at oklch 0.525 0.12 in light. That is where the three inks (foreground 0.10 / 0.95, foreground-light 0.39 / 0.80, foreground-lighter 0.52 / 0.68) and the page-toned dark dialogs come from. Button variants are in `packages/ui/src/components/Button/Button.tsx` (the default button is `raised-control-surface`, danger and warning are solid plates).
- **Dashboard**: the `signedIn` pages own `page`, both buttons, `input`, `field.label`, `card`, `shell`, the nav rows, `kbd`, both badges, `checkbox`, both switches, `alert` and the overlays (`command`, `menu`, `dialog`, `tooltip`). The organizations and General settings pages were first measured on 2026-10-21 through Remote Control; on 2026-10-22 Mehdi re-ran them with six more in a local session, light and dark through `prefers-color-scheme` (next-themes' `localStorage.theme` set to `system` for the run, then put back to `dark`), merged with `--merge --mode`. The organizations page steps into the first org's Projects page: "New project", the header's plan badge and the Status filter's checkbox. The `_` project ref now opens a project picker, so load the real project URL. Keys ending in `#…` are states of the General settings page: `#command` opens ⌘K, `#project-switcher` the header's project popover (a cmdk list in a Radix popover, Studio's menu surface; the avatar's account menu is the only `[role=menu]`), `#transfer-dialog` the Transfer project dialog (closed with Escape), `#rail-tooltip` a rail icon's tooltip, which holds "G then T" key chips. Nav rows read their ink from the inner `div`; badges are targeted without `text`, since a text match climbs to the enclosing link or button. The public sign-in page (`/dashboard/sign-in`) runs Studio too and agrees on fields (34px, r8, 13/450), full-ink labels and 26px r8 buttons; it stays a cross-check, since its controls sit on the page rather than in a card. Studio scales Tailwind's radius tokens by 4/3 (`apps/studio/styles/globals.css`: `--radius-md: 0.5rem`, `--radius-lg: 0.666667rem`), the design-system site doesn't, so docs parts leave out radius except pills and circles.
- **Kept from the docs**: `select.trigger` (the dashboard's only enabled select is the Data API schema picker, a multi-select with chips padded 6px), `tab` and `tab.selected` (no settings page has tabs; Advisors' card tabs keep one muted ink in both states), the segmented control (the dashboard's grid/list toggle is two icon-only radios with no track) and `checkbox.checked` (measuring it would mean toggling a filter).
- **The design-system docs** follow `prefers-color-scheme` (next-themes, `defaultTheme: "system"`); a stored `theme` key isn't needed, and setting one made both modes read the same. Each part targets the `.preview` demo, not the docs chrome; the docs' own `main h1` and `.mdx p` stand in for `heading.1` and `text`, which use the same theme variables.
- **Steps**: dashboard overlays open with `before` steps (⌘K, a click or a hover, then a wait for the content). `toggle.on` clicks the bold toggle, then hovers the page title so the row isn't read hovered. The segmented control is the toggle-group page's tiny "Segmented" example: its selected chip is a sliding `[data-segment-indicator]` beside the items, so `segmented.selected` reads the box from the indicator and the ink from the checked item.
- **Skips**: surfaces (`menu`, `command`, `dialog`, `card`) leave out color, which depends on their content. `menu.item` and `command.item` leave out padding: Supabase pads the row, the specimen pads inside the row's inset. Docs parts leave out radius (see Dashboard). `checkbox.checked` leaves out its edge: the gray `border-control` over the ink fill reads as rgb(2, 2, 2) / rgb(237, 239, 238), a line that doesn't show. `select.trigger` leaves out color. The dashboard `tooltip` leaves out height: the rail tooltip holds key chips, which make it 33px (the docs' plain tooltip is 30px). `kbd` is the header search's ⌘K chip and leaves out its edge: the search button's pill draws the outline around it, while the keys in ⌘K and tooltips wear a hairline. Tabs leave out radius, padding and shadow (line tabs draw their indicator apart from the box). `button.icon` is the split button's "More actions" half and leaves out radius (squared on one side); `segmented.selected` leaves out padding (the indicator has none); `progress.fill` leaves out radius (the track clips it). `heading.1` and `text` leave out size (docs typography, not app UI).
- **Sizes worth knowing** (dashboard; docs radii are ×3/4): buttons 26px r8 at 12/500, padded 10px (secondary 12px); fields 34px r8 at 13/450, on a 1.5% ink well with a gray edge that darkens on focus under a 2px mint ring at 55%, 2px out; labels 13/450 in full ink; checkbox 16px r5.33, checked in ink (the only dashboard checkbox sits in a popover, so its dark fill reads lighter than the docs' on the page); switch 34×20 with a 16px knob and a ringed track; product-menu rows 24.6px r8 padded 12px, at rest in foreground-light, current in full ink at 600 on #f3f3f3 / #1e1e1e; menus r8 with 28px r6 rows at 12/450, on oklch 0.24 in dark; tooltips r8, 12px padding, on the page tone; ⌘K r10.67, 44px r8 rows at 13/450, on the page tone; dialogs r10.67 on the page tone over `bg-black/40` with a slight blur; cards and alerts r10.67, cards with a low shadow; badges 17px pills, 9px uppercase at 500 with 0.63px tracking; `kbd` 17px r5.33, 11px at 450 with -0.275px tracking, in foreground-lighter, no fill in light. From the docs: line tabs 31px at 13/450; the segmented control has no track, its chip on the accent tone (#f3f3f3 / #1e1e1e) with no shadow, 26px items at 12/500; radios 16px, edged in the brand at rest too; toggles are shadcn's untouched default, quiet (no fill or edge) at 40px and 13/500; calendar days 36px at 13/450, the selected day on mint.
- **Surveyed, not measured**: project home, table editor, SQL editor, database tables, storage, logs, integrations, log drains and add-ons were captured in both modes for breadth (the editors and logs without shots: they show row data, queries and requests). Auth users, API and JWT keys, Vault and billing were skipped.
- **Not measured**: `checkbox.checked` on the dashboard, `calendar.today` (the docs calendar keeps today selected whatever is clicked; `calendar.selected` is the July example's selected day) and pagination (no live demo).
