# Supabase: sources

Supabase's `ui` package is open source ([supabase/supabase](https://github.com/supabase/supabase), `packages/ui`), and supabase.com/design-system renders it live. Values come from computed styles on those pages, cross-checked against the theme files.

## 2026-10-21

`tokens.json` comes from 14 pages of supabase.com/design-system, measured headless (`presets:measure --pack supabase`, probe d3433f1) at 1440×900, light and dark through `prefers-color-scheme`. Reference images, specimens and raw measures are in the project files under `presets/supabase/2026-10-21/`.

- **Theme files**: `packages/ui/build/css/themes/light.css` and `dark.css` define a runtime OKLCH theme on hue 157.5: `--surface` 0.995 / 0.19 with an `--elevation-step` of 0.024 / 0.025, `--foreground-lightness` 0.1 / 0.95 with muted and tertiary levels (0.65 / 0.8, 0.5 / 0.65), and `--primary` at oklch 0.525 0.12 in light. That is where the three inks (foreground 0.10 / 0.95, foreground-light 0.39 / 0.80, foreground-lighter 0.52 / 0.68) and the page-toned dark dialogs come from. Button variants are in `packages/ui/src/components/Button/Button.tsx` (the default button is `raised-control-surface`, danger and warning are solid plates).
- **The design-system docs** follow `prefers-color-scheme` (next-themes, `defaultTheme: "system"`); a stored `theme` key isn't needed, and setting one made both modes read the same. Each part targets the `.preview` demo, not the docs chrome; the docs' own `main h1` and `.mdx p` stand in for `heading.1` and `text`, which use the same theme variables.
- **Steps**: the `tooltip` (hover) and `command.item` / `command` (click "Open command menu") parts were measured by an ad-hoc Puppeteer pass that waits 1.5s after the step, then merged with `--merge`: the pack runner's settle loop reads `null` twice before Radix's open delay ends and records no match. Re-measure them the same way.
- **Skips**: surfaces (`shell`, `panel`, `menu`, `command`, `dialog`, `card`) leave out height and text, which depend on the demo's content. `menu`, `menu.item` and `command.item` leave out padding: Supabase pads the row, the specimen pads inside the row's inset. `checkbox` and `switch` leave out text, and `checkbox.checked` its edge. `select.trigger` leaves out color; `link` leaves out height. Tabs leave out radius, padding and shadow (line tabs draw their indicator apart from the box). `heading.1` and `text` leave out height and size (docs typography, not app UI).
- **Sizes worth knowing**: buttons 26px at 12/500 with 10px padding and r6; fields 34px at 13/450, r6, on a 1.5% ink well with a gray edge that darkens on focus under a 2px mint ring at 55%, 2px out; checkbox 16px r4, checked in ink; switch 34×20 with a 16px knob and a ringed track; line tabs 31px at 13/450; nav rows 32px r6 at 13/450, current at 500 on a 4% wash; menus r6 with 28px r4 rows at 12/450; tooltips 30px, r6, 12px padding on the page tone; dialogs r8; ⌘K 576×500, r8, a 56px search at 15px over 43px rows at 13/450, mono-caps group labels; badges 17px pills, 9px uppercase at 500 with 0.63px tracking; tables 40px mono-caps headers over 52px rows; cards r8 with a low shadow.
- **Not measured**: the signed-in dashboard (supabase.com/dashboard). It needs Mehdi's Chrome through Remote Control; asked in the review thread, not yet approved. The design-system pages render the same `ui` components the dashboard uses.
