# Supabase: sources

Supabase's `ui` package is open source ([supabase/supabase](https://github.com/supabase/supabase), `packages/ui`), and supabase.com/design-system renders it live. Values come from computed styles on those pages, cross-checked against the theme files.

## 2026-10-21

`tokens.json` comes from 21 pages of supabase.com/design-system, measured headless (`presets:measure --pack supabase`, probe 8eb9b12) at 1440×900, light and dark through `prefers-color-scheme`. Reference images, specimens and raw measures are in the project files under `presets/supabase/2026-10-21/`.

- **Theme files**: `packages/ui/build/css/themes/light.css` and `dark.css` define a runtime OKLCH theme on hue 157.5: `--surface` 0.995 / 0.19 with an `--elevation-step` of 0.024 / 0.025, `--foreground-lightness` 0.1 / 0.95 with muted and tertiary levels (0.65 / 0.8, 0.5 / 0.65), and `--primary` at oklch 0.525 0.12 in light. That is where the three inks (foreground 0.10 / 0.95, foreground-light 0.39 / 0.80, foreground-lighter 0.52 / 0.68) and the page-toned dark dialogs come from. Button variants are in `packages/ui/src/components/Button/Button.tsx` (the default button is `raised-control-surface`, danger and warning are solid plates).
- **The design-system docs** follow `prefers-color-scheme` (next-themes, `defaultTheme: "system"`); a stored `theme` key isn't needed, and setting one made both modes read the same. Each part targets the `.preview` demo, not the docs chrome; the docs' own `main h1` and `.mdx p` stand in for `heading.1` and `text`, which use the same theme variables.
- **Steps**: the `tooltip` (hover), `menu` and `command` parts open with `before` steps. `toggle.on` clicks the bold toggle, then hovers the page title so the row isn't read hovered. The segmented control is the toggle-group page's tiny "Segmented" example: its selected chip is a sliding `[data-segment-indicator]` beside the items, so `segmented.selected` reads the box from the indicator and the ink from the checked item.
- **Skips**: surfaces (`menu`, `command`, `dialog`, `card`) leave out color, which depends on the demo's content. `menu.item` and `command.item` leave out padding: Supabase pads the row, the specimen pads inside the row's inset. `checkbox.checked` leaves out its edge: the gray `border-control` over the ink fill reads as rgb(2, 2, 2) / rgb(237, 239, 238), a line that doesn't show. `select.trigger` leaves out color. Tabs leave out radius, padding and shadow (line tabs draw their indicator apart from the box). `button.icon` is the split button's "More actions" half and leaves out radius (squared on one side); `segmented.selected` leaves out padding (the indicator has none); `progress.fill` leaves out radius (the track clips it). `heading.1` and `text` leave out size (docs typography, not app UI).
- **Not on the pages**: pagination has no live demo. The calendar demo keeps today selected whatever is clicked, so `calendar.today` isn't measured; `calendar.selected` is the July example's selected day.
- **Sizes worth knowing**: buttons 26px at 12/500 with 10px padding and r6; fields 34px at 13/450, r6, on a 1.5% ink well with a gray edge that darkens on focus under a 2px mint ring at 55%, 2px out; checkbox 16px r4, checked in ink; switch 34×20 with a 16px knob and a ringed track; line tabs 31px at 13/450; nav rows 32px r6 at 13/450, current at 500 on a 4% wash; menus r6 with 28px r4 rows at 12/450; tooltips 30px, r6, 12px padding on the page tone; dialogs r8; ⌘K 576×500, r8, a 56px search at 15px over 43px rows at 13/450, mono-caps group labels; badges 17px pills, 9px uppercase at 500 with 0.63px tracking; tables 40px mono-caps headers over 52px rows; cards r8 with a low shadow; field labels 13/450 in foreground-light; radios 16px, edged in the brand at rest too; the segmented control has no track, its chip on the accent tone (#f3f3f3 / #1e1e1e) with no shadow, 26px items at 12/500; toggles are shadcn's untouched default, quiet (no fill or edge) at 40px and 13/500; calendar days 36px at 13/450, the selected day on mint; alerts r8 on a faint page-toned fill.
- **Not measured**: the signed-in dashboard (supabase.com/dashboard). It needs Mehdi's Chrome through Remote Control; asked in the review thread, not yet approved. The design-system pages render the same `ui` components the dashboard uses.
