# Airbnb: sources

Airbnb publishes no design tokens as code, but every page ships its DLS palette as CSS variables (`--palette-*`), so colors come from those and sizes from computed styles.

## 2026-10-21

All public, signed out, headless Chromium at 1440×900, DPR 2, light only (`prefers-color-scheme` doesn't switch the guest site). Raw measures and screenshots are in the project files under `presets/airbnb/2026-10-21/`. Probe 8eb9b12.

- **Pages.** `www.airbnb.com/` (tabs, the header's quiet and icon buttons, the main menu, the login dialog's field, the search panel's Dates | Flexible switch), `/s/Paris/homes` (the page numbers; with Filters open, the ink confirm, the dialog and the amenity chips) and a public listing, `/rooms/47803806` (soft secondary button, focus ring, the booking card, calendar days, headings, body and muted text). Nothing in the pack reads listing or host content beyond the element's style.
- **Hydration.** Airbnb renders before React attaches, so clicks do nothing for 3–4s after `load`. Every page settles 4000ms; Filters, the profile menu and the login dialog open with `focus` + `press: Enter`, and the When field and the Wifi chip take a `click` after the settle. The whole pack runs with `--pack`, nothing merged. `input.focus` focuses the field's input in a `before` step: `focus: true` on the part did not trigger Airbnb's focus style. `toggle.on` clicks the chip instead of pressing Enter, which would leave a focus ring on it.
- **Font stand-in.** `tokens.json` maps Cereal (`airbnb cereal vf`) to Plus Jakarta Sans, so `fontFamily` rows read as stand-in.
- **Palette.** `:root` of the HTML holds the light palette, `client.css` the `-dark` set the guest site never applies. Light: page `#ffffff`, ink `#222222`, muted `#6c6c6c`, hairline `#dddddd` (`border-secondary`), field edge `#8c8c8c`, soft fill `#f2f2f2` / `#f7f7f7`, track `#ebebeb`, error `#c13515` on `#fff5f3`, success `#038026`, warning `#eb6100`, scrim black 30%.
- **Dark is token-derived.** The guest site ships no dark mode, so `dark` in `tokens.json` is written by hand from the `-dark` palette, on the light entries' props, with clear fills composited over the page and no shadows (the `-dark` set has none): page and overlays `#111111`, ink `#f7f7f7`, values `#efefef`, muted `#a6a6a6`, hairline `#2c2c2c`, field edge `#6a6a6a`, soft fill `#222222`, inverse `#f7f7f7`, scrim black 60%.
- **Brand.** Rausch `#ff385c` is the logo and `bg-primary-core`. The CTAs (Reserve, login Continue, Search) are a gradient: `#e61e4d → #e31c5f → #d70466` in light, `#ff385c → #ff497d` in dark; the price bars and the gradient's midpoint are `#e31c5f`, the search icon `#da1249`. The probe reads `background-color` only, so the gradient is recorded here, not in `tokens.json`.
- **Two primaries.** Dialog confirms ("Show 1,000+ places") are ink `#222222` / `#f7f7f7` at 40px, r12, 14/500, 20px padding; conversion CTAs are the Rausch gradient at 48px, 16/500. `button.primary` measures the ink confirm.
- **Weights against the stand-in font.** Cereal is proprietary. Rendered side by side in a canvas, Cereal 500 carries 3.50 px of ink per px of width at 14px; Plus Jakarta Sans 500 carries 3.11, 600 carries 3.58 (5.53 vs 5.64 at 22px). So the preset's 600 labels and headings match Cereal's 500, and the `fontWeight` rows stay off by 100 on purpose.
- **Skips.** `height` and `fontSize` where the part's size is set by its page (headings, the secondary button's long label), the tabs' box (the label span is measured), the quiet button's radius (clear at rest, a pill only on hover), padding on the field (the label and input pad different boxes), `boxShadow` on fields (the edge is an inset shadow; `edge` compares it), the dialog's `edge` (a 2% black ring, `#fafafa` on white) and the card's fill (the booking card sits on the page).
- **Segmented, two styles.** `segmented` measures the search panel's Dates | Flexible switch: a 44px `#ebebeb` track at 100px corners, 36px chips in ink 14/500, the selected one white inside a 1px `#dddddd` edge, no shadow. The Filters dialog's Type of place is the other style: a 1px `#dddddd` r16 track, 54px, whose selected cell is `#f7f7f7` with a 2px `#222222` edge at r12, drawn by a sibling element, so it is noted here and not in the pack.
- **Chips.** `toggle` is the Filters dialog's Wifi chip: 48px, r28, 16px padding, 14/400, white in a 1px `#dddddd` edge. On, the fill stays white and the edge turns ink, drawn as a 1px border plus a 1px inset ring; `edge` reads the border's 1px, `boxShadow` the ring.
- **Measured, not in the pack** (no specimen part): the number stepper's 32px `#f2f2f2` circles; calendar days at 48px in the search panel (the pack reads the listing's 42px), past days grayed, single-letter weekdays; tags 20px, r6, 11/500, `#6c6c6c` on `#f2f2f2`; tabs 14/500 with a 3px `#222222` bar under the icon and label and no rule under the list; menu rows 36px at 14/400 with 24px inline padding and separators inset 24px; dialogs 568px wide with a centered title.
- **Not measured.** `field.label`: the login field's label floats inside the field at 16/400 `#6c6c6c`, not above it. No public radio or progress bar.
- **No public checkbox or switch.** The AI search toggle is a custom glass switch, not the DLS one; the checkbox, switch, command and drawer sheets are inferred.
