# Airbnb: sources

Airbnb publishes no design tokens as code, but every page ships its DLS palette as CSS variables (`--palette-*`), so colors come from those and sizes from computed styles.

## 2026-10-21

All public, signed out, headless Chromium at 1440×900, DPR 2, light only (`prefers-color-scheme` doesn't switch the guest site). Raw measures and screenshots are in the project files under `presets/airbnb/2026-10-21/`. Probe 89a5551.

- **Pages.** `www.airbnb.com/` (tabs, the header's quiet button, the main menu, the login dialog's field, muted text), `/s/Paris/homes` with Filters open (the ink confirm, the dialog) and a public listing, `/rooms/47803806` (soft secondary button, focus ring, the booking card, headings, body text). Nothing in the pack reads listing or host content beyond the element's style.
- **Hydration.** Airbnb renders before React attaches, so a part's `before` step must wait for hydration (3–4s after `load`): `element.click()` and `page.click()` on Filters and the profile button do nothing, focus + Enter opens them. The `--pack` runner reads too early on this site; `input`, `input.focus` and `button.primary` were measured with a scripted run (focus + Enter, then a 3s wait) and merged with `--merge`.
- **Palette.** `:root` of the HTML holds the light palette, `client.css` the `-dark` set the guest site never applies. Light: page `#ffffff`, ink `#222222`, muted `#6c6c6c`, hairline `#dddddd` (`border-secondary`), field edge `#8c8c8c`, soft fill `#f2f2f2` / `#f7f7f7`, track `#ebebeb`, error `#c13515` on `#fff5f3`, success `#038026`, warning `#eb6100`, scrim black 30%.
- **Dark is token-derived.** The guest site ships no dark mode, so `dark` in `tokens.json` is written by hand from the `-dark` palette, on the light entries' props, with clear fills composited over the page and no shadows (the `-dark` set has none): page and overlays `#111111`, ink `#f7f7f7`, values `#efefef`, muted `#a6a6a6`, hairline `#2c2c2c`, field edge `#6a6a6a`, soft fill `#222222`, inverse `#f7f7f7`, scrim black 60%.
- **Brand.** Rausch `#ff385c` is the logo and `bg-primary-core`. The CTAs (Reserve, login Continue, Search) are a gradient: `#e61e4d → #e31c5f → #d70466` in light, `#ff385c → #ff497d` in dark; the price bars and the gradient's midpoint are `#e31c5f`, the search icon `#da1249`. The probe reads `background-color` only, so the gradient is recorded here, not in `tokens.json`.
- **Two primaries.** Dialog confirms ("Show 1,000+ places") are ink `#222222` / `#f7f7f7` at 40px, r12, 14/500, 20px padding; conversion CTAs are the Rausch gradient at 48px, 16/500. `button.primary` measures the ink confirm.
- **Weights against the stand-in font.** Cereal is proprietary. Rendered side by side in a canvas, Cereal 500 carries 3.50 px of ink per px of width at 14px; Plus Jakarta Sans 500 carries 3.11, 600 carries 3.58 (5.53 vs 5.64 at 22px). So the preset's 600 labels and headings match Cereal's 500, and the `fontWeight` rows stay off by 100 on purpose.
- **Skips.** `borderColor` everywhere but `card` (it is `currentColor` on borderless parts), `fontFamily` (Cereal), `height` and `fontSize` where the part's size is set by its page (headings, body text, the dialog), padding where the two sides pad different boxes (`menu`), `boxShadow` on fields (the edge is an inset shadow; `edge` compares it).
- **Measured, not in the pack** (no specimen part yet): toggle chips 48px pills with a 1px `#dddddd` edge at 14/400 (the search filter chips are smaller); the number stepper's 32px `#f2f2f2` circles; segmented controls two ways (Dates | Flexible: a white raised pill on an `#ebebeb` pill track; Type of place: a 1px `#dddddd` r16 track with a 2px `#222222` r12 selected cell); calendar days 48px circles (42px on the listing), past days grayed, single-letter weekdays; tags 20px, r6, 11/500, `#6c6c6c` on `#f2f2f2`; tabs 14/500 with a 3px `#222222` bar under the icon and label and no rule under the list; the header's icon buttons 40px `#f2f2f2` circles; menu rows 36px at 14/400 with 24px inline padding and separators inset 24px; dialogs 568px wide with a centered title.
- **No public checkbox or switch.** The AI search toggle is a custom glass switch, not the DLS one; the checkbox, switch, command and drawer sheets are inferred.
